Help / Frequently Asked Questions

What is Fishers Student Exchange?

Fishers Student Exchange is a website where Fishers students can exchange, sell, or find school-related items and resources.

How do I post an item?

Go to the exchange page and follow the instructions to add your item.

Who can use Fishers Student Exchange?

Fishers students can use the website to find and exchange items with other students.

How do I contact another student?

Use the contact information provided with the item or post.

What if I have a problem?

If you have a problem with the website or an exchange, contact the website administrator for help.
