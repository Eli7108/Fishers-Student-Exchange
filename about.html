About Us

Welcome to Fishers Student Exchange! We are a student-run website where students can buy and sell old school supplies that they no longer need. Instead of letting used notebooks, calculators, binders, and other supplies go to waste, students can list them here and sell them to other students who need them. It's an easy, affordable, and eco-friendly way to shop for school supplies right in the Fishers community.

[Image placeholder: photo of students exchanging school supplies]

Our Mission

Our mission is to make school supplies more affordable for students while reducing waste. We believe that a barely-used calculator or a half-full pack of folders shouldn't end up in a landfill when another student could use it. Fishers Student Exchange connects buyers and sellers so everyone saves money and helps the environment.

[Image placeholder: photo or graphic representing recycling/sustainability]

How It Works

Sellers list the school supplies they no longer need, along with a price and description. Buyers browse the listings, find what they need, and connect with the seller to complete the exchange. It's simple, fast, and made by students, for students.
